## Character invariants

- Orange fox and cream cheeks; dark rounded glasses and a consistent rounded face.
- Scholarly, curious, rigorous and supportive personality.
- Consistent anatomy and proportions across all seven reviewer skills.
- Never replace the base character with a new animal or a journal's trademark.

## Discipline system

- **Accent:** #8465A5
- **Dark ink:** #4B365F
- **Pale ground:** #E8DEF2
- **Functional emphasis:** Structure · properties · mechanism
- **Tagline:** Separate measured properties from proposed mechanisms.
- **Evidence question:** Does the characterization prove the mechanism?
