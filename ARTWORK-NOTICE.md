# Artwork, provenance and approval

## Design status

This repository contains V0.2 **draft concepts** and project-level visual specifications. No draft automatically becomes an official GeoGeekLab product mark.

## Canonical mascots

The source of truth for RefFox, GeoMole, Scout, Evi, Geo Gecko and Red Panda remains their respective project repository. They are linked in [GALLERY.md](GALLERY.md). This central repository does **not** relicense, copy over or silently replace those originals.

## Concept assets

- `family/ref-fox/**` — reviewer-variant layout and symbol studies; not master RefFox drawings.
- `new-mascots/**` — provisional characters requiring design approval.
- `logos/**` — preliminary scientific-symbol proposals requiring scientific semantic review.
- AI-produced moodboards are inspiration only; they are not official logos, technical drawings or a substitute for editable masters.

## Release checklist

1. Preserve the original character’s identity anchors and verify artwork provenance.
2. Verify scientific correctness and accessibility (especially color dependence).
3. Test silhouette and readability at 32, 64 and 128 pixels.
4. Produce monochrome, light and dark variants plus app and social formats.
5. Record approval by project name, asset checksum and version before adoption.
6. Get any necessary rights clearance before redistribution or external branding.
